Derive ordinary published-event discovery from registration-option eligibility
and remove audience state from events, templates, and platform operations.
Anonymous visitors use tenant default-user roles only for ordinary options and
must sign in before registration. Optionless announcements keep their separate
signed-in role targeting and link-only empty state. Direct links preserve
explicit anonymous sign-in and signed-in ineligibility states, and paid
registration completion compensates a payment when current role eligibility has
been lost.
