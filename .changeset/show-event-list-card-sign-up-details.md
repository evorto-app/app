Show how full an event is, the price, the sign-up opening time and organizer slots on event list cards. Members see a fill level ("Many free spots", "Some spots left", "Few spots left", "Event is full"); people who may organize the event see the exact number of spots left. Not-yet-open and closed events are labelled on the card, so members and organizers can pick events without opening each one.
