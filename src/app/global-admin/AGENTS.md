# Global administration

- Keep this area limited to organization configuration, email delivery outcomes,
  platform change history, and payment/refund recovery.
- Link to an organization's own website for events, templates, members, roles,
  tax rates, scanning, receipts, and routine finance. Global administrators
  already have full capabilities on those tenant-scoped pages without membership.
- Do not introduce parallel platform editors for ordinary tenant workflows.
- Keep payment recovery target-scoped, reasoned, and audited. Retain confirmed
  outcomes and uncertain writes across failed or paused follow-up reads; never
  repeat a write automatically to compensate for a missing response.
- A link to the current organization uses its current origin, including local
  development. Other organizations use their validated canonical HTTPS origin.
